5 Fair Judging Tips

  • Score the app's potential, not just current polish
  • Use the full 1-10 range - avoid clustering around 7
  • Consider the team's skill level (1st year vs final year)
  • Focus on problem-solution fit, not just technical complexity
  • Add brief notes to help teams improve
  • If unsure, re-watch the demo (it's recorded)

⚠️ Important

Do not score your own institution's submissions. Flag any conflicts of interest to the admin immediately.
